# Evidence: ablation, one mechanism off at a time (evaluation test 10, 2026-10-24)

Script: `bench/ablation.sh` (`RUNS=5`). Each mechanism off against the full system, alternating in one
session, on the workload where it should matter. One instance, closed loop (12 connections × 16 in
flight), Zipf keys (exponent 0.99), servers on CPUs 0-5 and the load generator on 6-11, FLUSHALL and
10,000 plans seeded before every run. 80 runs in all, no failed call and no `UNVERIFIED` answer.

## How each mechanism is switched off

| Mechanism | Off by |
| --- | --- |
| lease | the strict engine |
| early refill | `RL_ABLATE=early_refill` |
| adaptive chunk | `RL_ABLATE=adaptive_chunk` (the chunk stays at the cap) |
| alignment | a build with `-DRL_LEASE_ALIGN=8` (`build/noalign`), else the same as release |
| batcher | off by default: the row compares `RL_BATCHER=adaptive` with the default |
| deny cache | `RL_ABLATE=deny_cache` |
| plan cache | `RL_PLAN_TTL_MS=0` (the plan is read on every check) |

## Results

Medians of 5 runs; "with" is the full system (for the batcher, the batcher on):

| Mechanism | Workload | With: checks/s, p99 | Without: checks/s, p99 | Change without it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| lease | `api_default`, 1M keys | 73,172, 6.05 ms | 51,434, 8.55 ms | **−29.7%** |
| early refill | same | 74,571, 5.79 ms | 73,927, 5.79 ms | −0.9% (noise) |
| adaptive chunk | same | 73,715, 6.05 ms | 74,768, 5.79 ms | +1.4% (noise) |
| alignment | same | 73,208, 6.05 ms | 74,904, 6.05 ms | +2.3% (noise) |
| batcher | strict path, 1M keys | 51,457, 7.84 ms | 50,984, 8.93 ms | −0.9% (noise) |
| deny cache | `customer`, Free customers out of quota, 10k keys | 98,128, 3.92 ms | 34,706, 13.78 ms | **−64.6%** |
| plan cache | `customer`, Pay customers, 10k keys | 42,232, 10.17 ms | 34,148, 11.09 ms | **−19.1%** |

The four rows marked noise have overlapping ranges:

| Mechanism | With: range | Without: range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| early refill | 70,781 to 75,442 | 70,891 to 74,535 |
| adaptive chunk | 68,703 to 75,408 | 70,773 to 75,717 |
| alignment | 69,181 to 75,446 | 65,838 to 75,255 |
| batcher (on: with) | 49,145 to 51,770 | 47,212 to 52,170 |

## Reading

- **Three mechanisms carry the throughput:**
  - *the lease:* +42% over strict on Zipf keys;
  - *the deny cache:* 2.8 times, for customers out of quota, and p99 from 13.8 to 3.9 ms;
  - *the plan cache:* +24%.
- **Four have no measurable throughput effect on one instance, which is not what they are for:**
  - *Early refill* is for latency on a lease about to run dry, when demand is close to the refill
    rate. Here leases rarely run dry.
  - *The adaptive chunk* is for accuracy and for tokens stranded on idle instances (the bound of
    `burst_bound_2026-10-22.md` uses the cap), not for speed.
  - *Alignment* was 14 times faster in the lease microbenchmark at 16 threads
    (`results/2026-10-18_lease_microbench`). In the server, a check also pays for gRPC, so false
    sharing between leases is too small to measure.
  - *The batcher* gains nothing, as in Week 3 (`results/2026-10-18_batcher`). It stays off by default.
- **A first deny-cache run measured nothing (+0.9%)** and is kept in `summary.txt`. A Free customer is
  held to its burst of 2 a second, so it cannot reach its quota of 200 within a 15 s run, and the
  cache only acts on a customer out of quota. The rerun (`summary_deny_cache.txt`) starts every
  Free customer at 200 used.
